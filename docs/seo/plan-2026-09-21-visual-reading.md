Owner request: make the existing origin story inviting to read, with graphics,
a meme, diagrams and pull quotes; audit and fix Observatory and Skills SEO/AEO.
Canonical addresses, personal testimony, reviewed aggregate and private-data
boundaries remain fixed. Skills remains the family site's primary purpose.

## Reader contract before implementation

Surface: public article and landing; job: understand why the tool exists, how a
credential can be copied, what the observed count means, and how to start.
Falsifiers: chart implies 202 unique stolen keys; diagram implies a reconstructed
incident; figures require JS or horizontal scrolling at 320px; an empty URL returns
the home page with HTTP 200; the article opens with a full viewport of decoration
before giving the reader its subject.

Use the existing Field Notes palette and add a documented editorial type role:
Georgia/system serif body, sans headings and diagrams. User's Medium reference
means readable measure, generous leading, byline, deck, section links and editorial
figures, not a copied Medium brand or interface. Variance 5 (diagram, chart, quote,
cartoon serve different passages), motion 1 (static), density 4 (one text column,
figures at natural breaks). Fixed composition direction, no competing-cast fork.
Two bounded visual critique passes at 1440px and 390/320px; then fix only concrete
findings. Native web markup/DOM checks cover implementation and verification.
No mobile-native UI or design-editor handoff applies. Assistive-technology and
full WCAG certification are not claimed; scoped DOM, keyboard and contrast checks
will be recorded separately from visual judgement.
